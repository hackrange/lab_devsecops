Everything starts on its own. Stop what you are not using to free up memory.

Start over

If something is broken and you cannot tell what, put the whole lab back the way a fresh install left it.

This deletes everything you made in the lab: your repositories and pipelines on the Git server, your package registry rules, your findings, everything in your Kubernetes cluster, and your Keycloak, Kong and Grafana setup. Your lab desktop restarts with a new password. Copy anything you want to keep somewhere else (for example to GitHub) first. It takes a few minutes.

Your lab desktop

The workstation you are working in. It is disposable, like the hosted lab: push your work to the lab Git server before you run hackrange-lab stop or hackrange-lab reset on your Ubuntu machine.
